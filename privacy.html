Privacy Policy

What the app accesses. Champions League Bot uses Google Drive and Google Docs permissions limited to files it creates itself (the drive.file and documents scopes). It cannot see any other file in your Google account.

What it does with data. It creates documents, writes text into them, reads comments left on them, and shares them with named collaborators chosen by the account owner. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Storage. Authentication tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer. No data is stored on any server operated by the app.

Contact. Questions can be raised with the account owner directly; the app has a single user.
