Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Fitness Assistant is a personal app used only by its owner. It connects to the WHOOP API so the owner can see recovery, strain, sleep, workouts, and basic body stats, log lifts and runs, and get a short end-of-day recap.

What this app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app requests these WHOOP scopes:

  • Recovery, including recovery score, heart rate variability, and resting heart rate
  • Cycles, including day strain
  • Sleep
  • Workouts recorded by WHOOP
  • Profile, including name and email
  • Body measurements, including height, weight, and max heart rate

The owner may also type workout notes into the app, such as lifts, sets, runs, and how a session felt. The app does not access anyone else's WHOOP account.

How the data is used

Data is used only to help the owner train: plan a session, adjust volume when they feel weaker, track running, and summarize the day. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with other people or companies except WHOOP, which already holds the source data, and the infrastructure that stores it for the owner.

How long it is kept

Data is kept so the owner can look back at training. The owner can ask for it to be deleted. Disconnecting the app in WHOOP stops new data from being pulled. Deleting the WHOOP developer app revokes access.

Security

The WHOOP client secret and access tokens are stored as secrets and are not put in chat logs or shared. Access is limited to the owner.

WHOOP

WHOOP collects and stores the underlying health data under its own privacy policy at whoop.com/us/en/privacy. This policy covers only what Fitness Assistant does with data the owner authorizes.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the contact email listed on the Fitness Assistant app in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard.
